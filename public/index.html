Why use Kryptik?

Welcome to Kryptik Passcodes. In a sea of password generators and managers, we aim to stand out from the crowd. With our easy to use strength checker you can check how secure your existing passwords are. If our system detects that you may be putting yourself at risk using weaker passwords, we will ask if you would like to generate a strong randomly generated password. If you are happy with your current password simply click no and your old password stays intact.

We understand that secure randomly generated passwords can be difficult to remember. So we have implemented our zero-knowledge encrypted vaults, all you need to do is create a free account. Sign in and start saving your passwords, usernames, and website urls to your vault.

What is zero-knowledge encryption? This is our way of ensuring that nobody, can view your credentials but you. Not even us.

Can you still check password strength without an account?Yes! Everyone deserves to know if they are putting themselves at risk.
